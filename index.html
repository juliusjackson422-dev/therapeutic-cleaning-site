Questions

Before you book.

Anything not here, call and ask. We'd rather answer it now than on the doorstep.

Do I need to be home?
No. Most clients give us a key, a code or a lockbox after the first visit. If you'd rather be there the first time to walk us through the house, that's the usual way to start.
What do I need to do before you arrive?
Clear the floors and the counters of anything personal you'd rather we didn't move — that's it. We don't need the house tidied first. Clearing surfaces just means more of the visit goes on actual cleaning.
What products do you use?
Low-scent products we bring with us, chosen so the house doesn't smell of cleaning afterwards. If you have allergies, asthma, pets or a surface that needs something specific, tell us and we'll work to it — or use yours.
How much does it cost?
We quote per visit, after seeing the house — photos or a ten-minute look. A three-bedroom on a weekly rhythm and the same house on a one-off deep clean are different jobs, and quoting either blind usually means someone's disappointed.
What if something isn't right?
Tell us within 48 hours and we come back and redo that part at no charge. This is a small operation — it matters that you do not have to chase it.
Which areas do you cover?
Mostly houses and apartments within about a twenty-minute drive, plus the occasional small office. If you're a little further out, ask anyway — it depends on the day and what else is on the schedule.
